# Naval

152 sources. About 278411 words. The notebook's name is Naval. The long episode calls him the co-founder of AngelList, on Nivi's podcast.

## The argument, as these sources tell it

1. **Wealth is not money, and it is not status.** Wealth is assets that earn while you sleep. Money is how wealth is transferred. Status is rank in a social hierarchy, and he says wealth is not zero-sum. → [Wealth](concepts/wealth.md)
2. **You cannot get that by renting hours.** You need specific knowledge, the kind the tweetstorm keeps having to explain, and you need equity. If you can sell and build, he says you will be unstoppable. → [Specific Knowledge](concepts/specific-knowledge.md)
3. **Leverage multiplies judgment.** He names three classes. Labor and capital are the old ones. Products and media that can be copied at no marginal cost are the new one. Accountability, under your own name, is how society hands you equity and leverage. → [Leverage](concepts/leverage.md)
4. **The returns are compound.** Long-term games with long-term people are where compound interest shows up, in relationships, money, and learning. → [Long-term Games](concepts/long-term.md)
5. **Wanting has a price, and reading is a loop.** Desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until you get the thing. Reading starts wherever you already are: read what you love until you love to read. → [Desire and Reading](concepts/desire.md)

The notebook also holds long conversations, among them David Deutsch across several files, Vitalik on Ethereum, Matt Ridley on innovation, Kapil Gupta, and a Periscope from May 2020.
