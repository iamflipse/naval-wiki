# About

This book is the notebook titled Naval. It holds 152 sources and about 278411 words. The list is [All 152 Sources](sources.md).

How to Get Rich names the show as the navall podcast, the host as Nivi, and the subject as the co-founder of AngelList. The caption spells the show "navall" and the company "angel lists." The surname is not in these sources, so this book uses the notebook's name, Naval.

Many of the shorter files are that same tweetstorm, How to Get Rich Without Getting Lucky, split into episodes. How to Get Rich is Nivi's collected cut of those episodes, about three and a half hours in his introduction. The same sentence can appear in a short file and in the long one. A quote on a concept page is tied to the file under it.

Three files are in the notebook and are not readable English. They are marked on the catalog. Their titles are not treated as things he said.

A paraphrase is marked. A block quote was checked against that source.
