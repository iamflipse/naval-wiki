# Specific Knowledge

**The knowledge that pays is the part he says people keep misunderstanding.**

> "specific knowledge is probably the hardest thing to get across in this whole tweet storm"
>
> [Arm Yourself With Specific Knowledge](sources/arm-yourself-with-specific-knowledge.md).

Arm Yourself With Specific Knowledge says the set you need is specific knowledge, accountability, leverage, and judgment. He says he will throw judgment in even if that particular tweet left it out. Specific knowledge, in the same passage, is tied to getting paid, and he says the confusion is the idea that everything can be taught. The file goes on. This page keeps the warning: if it is a standard curriculum, it is probably not the thing he means.

Renting hours is the path he tells you to leave.

> "you must own equity a piece of a business to gain your financial freedom"
>
> [You Won't Get Rich Renting Out Your Time](sources/you-wont-get-rich-renting-out-your-time.md).

You Won't Get Rich Renting Out Your Time says people think work itself creates wealth, and that this probably will not work. The piece you need is equity. The caption says "renting of your time." The title says "renting out."

The skill pair he names is sell and build.

> "learn to sell learn to build if you can do both you will be unstoppable"
>
> [Learn to Sell, Learn to Build](sources/learn-to-sell-learn-to-build.md).

Learn to Sell, Learn to Build calls building the product the hard category, and says it is multivariate: design, development, manufacturing, and more. Selling is the other broad category. Both, he says, and you are unstoppable. One of them is not the claim.
