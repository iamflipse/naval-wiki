# Leverage

**Judgment matters because leverage multiplies it, and leverage is no longer only other people and money.**

> "leverage is a force multiplier for your judgment"
>
> [Judgment Is the Decisive Skill](sources/judgment-is-the-decisive-skill.md).

Judgment Is the Decisive Skill says this is an age of nearly infinite leverage, and that the great fortunes are created through it. The first job, in that file, is to obtain leverage. Permission is one route he names there. The file continues into what permissionless leverage is.

The three classes are named in the short file.

> "there are three broad classes of Leverage"
>
> [The Three Forms of Leverage](sources/the-three-forms-of-leverage.md).

Labor is other humans working for you. He calls it the oldest form, great in the old world, and a status game in the modern one: everyone is trying to become the lead monkey, so having people work for you is difficult. Capital is the second old form. The Power of Leverage quotes Archimedes on a lever long enough to move the Earth, and says humans are not built to understand leverage because our past maps inputs to outputs one to one.

The new form is a product you can copy without adding cost.

> "products that have no marginal cost of replication"
>
> [Product and Media Are New Leverage](sources/product-and-media-are-new-leverage.md).

Product and Media Are New Leverage dates that to the last few hundred years: the printing press, then broadcast, then the internet and code. You multiply effort without other humans and without raising money from someone else.

Accountability is how he says you get permission to use the older leverage, and a claim on the result.

> "embrace accountability and take business risks under your own name"
>
> [Embrace Accountability to Get Leverage](sources/embrace-accountability-to-get-leverage.md).

Embrace Accountability to Get Leverage says society will reward that with responsibility, equity, and leverage. The risk is under your name. That is the price of the equity in [Specific Knowledge](specific-knowledge.md).
