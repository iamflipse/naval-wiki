# Glossary

Labels as these sources use them. Caption spelling is kept when the quote is the source.

- **Wealth.** Assets that earn while you sleep. Not zero-sum, in his house example.
- **Money.** How wealth is transferred. Social credits and debits on other people's time.
- **Status.** Your ranking in the social hierarchy.
- **Specific knowledge.** The part of the tweetstorm he says is hardest to get across. Not the idea that everything can be taught.
- **Equity.** A piece of a business. What he says you need instead of renting out your time.
- **Sell and build.** The two broad categories. Both, and he says you will be unstoppable.
- **Leverage.** A force multiplier for judgment. Three classes: labor, capital, and products or media.
- **Labor.** Other people working for you. Old leverage, and a status game in his telling.
- **Product leverage.** Products with no marginal cost of replication. Printing press, broadcast, code.
- **Accountability.** Business risks under your own name. Society, he says, answers with responsibility, equity, and leverage.
- **Compound interest.** The benefit of long-term games with long-term people, in relationships, money, and learning.
- **Desire.** A contract with yourself to be unhappy until you get what you want.
- **navall.** The caption's spelling of the podcast. Nivi is the host. The subject is called the co-founder of angel lists.
