# Wealth

**Wealth, money, and status are three different things in the tweetstorm.**

Seek Wealth, Not Money or Status, and the collected How to Get Rich episode, walk the same distinction.

> "wealth is assets that earn while you sleep"
>
> [Seek Wealth, Not Money or Status](sources/seek-wealth-not-money-or-status.md).

He keeps going: the factory, the robots, the program running at night and serving customers. The thing you want is the asset that works when you are not in the room.

> "money is how we transfer wealth"
>
> [Seek Wealth, Not Money or Status](sources/seek-wealth-not-money-or-status.md).

Money, in that stretch, is social credit. It is the ability to have credits and debits on other people's time. Society owes you something if you created value. Money is the record of that, not the factory.

> "status is just your ranking in the social hierarchy"
>
> [Seek Wealth, Not Money or Status](sources/seek-wealth-not-money-or-status.md).

> "wealth is not a zero-sum game"
>
> [Seek Wealth, Not Money or Status](sources/seek-wealth-not-money-or-status.md).

His example is a house. Everybody can have one. Your house does not take away his. Status, a rank, does not work that way. The title of the episode is the instruction: seek the assets, not the rank and not the tokens.

The host of that episode says the tweets are the storm called how to get rich without getting lucky. How to Get Rich uses the same phrase.

> "how to get rich without getting lucky"
>
> [How to Get Rich](sources/how-to-get-rich.md).
