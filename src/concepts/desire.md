# Desire and Reading

**Two practices sit beside the money tweets. One is about wanting. One is about what you read.**

> "desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until you get what you want"
>
> [Desire Is a Contract You Make to Be Unhappy](sources/desire-is-a-contract-you-make-to-be-unhappy.md).

Desire Is a Contract You Make to Be Unhappy says you become disturbed because you want something, you work to get it and you are miserable in the meantime, and when you get it you revert. The contract is with yourself. The unhappiness is the term of the contract, not a side effect he forgot.

A file titled Happiness Is a Skill You Can Develop is in the notebook. Its caption is not readable enough to quote. The title is the title. This page does not pretend the body states the skill.

Reading, in the file that is readable, is a loop.

> "read what you love until you love to read"
>
> [Read What You Love Until You Love to Read](sources/read-what-you-love-until-you-love-to-read.md).

Read What You Love Until You Love to Read says everyone he knows who reads a lot loves to read, because they read books they loved. He calls it a catch-22 and tells you to start where you are. A separate short file, Read the Best 100 Books Over and Over Again, is 89 words. The catalog has it. The sentence above is the one checked here.
